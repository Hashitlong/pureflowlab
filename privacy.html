Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information is involved when you visit PureFlow Lab (pureflowlab.com). PureFlow Lab is published by Mooresville Marketing.

What we collect

We don’t run analytics, forms, accounts or a newsletter on this site. We don’t ask you for personal information to read it.

If you email us at support@mooresvillemarketing.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message, and use it only to reply.

Third parties involved when you visit

  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify processes standard technical request data (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver and secure the site, under its own privacy policy.
  • Fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Amazon. PureFlow Lab is an Amazon Associate. If you click a link to Amazon, Amazon may set cookies to attribute any qualifying purchase to this site. That happens on Amazon’s site under Amazon’s privacy notice. We receive aggregated sales reports, not your personal details.

Cookies

PureFlow Lab itself doesn’t set cookies. Third parties listed above may, on their own sites or services.

We don’t sell personal information

We don’t sell or share personal information for advertising.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser, and you can ask us to delete any email correspondence by writing to support@mooresvillemarketing.com.

Children

This site isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if we add analytics), we’ll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions: support@mooresvillemarketing.com
